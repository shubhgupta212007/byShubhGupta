02

The simple setup.
The surprising result.

In classical physics, a ball could have any energy in a box. A quantum particle is different: its wave must fit neatly between the walls, like a plucked string.

ALLOWED WAVEψn(x) = √2L sin(nπxL)

01

No zero-energy state. Even the lowest wave has curvature, so the particle has a minimum energy: the zero-point energy.

02

Nodes are forbidden spots. The nth state has n − 1 interior nodes where the probability of finding the particle is zero.

03

Small box, big energy gaps. Energy scales as 1/L²—halve the width and each level becomes four times higher.
